GutEquity Risk Index

Ancestry-informed risk stratification for inflammatory bowel disease, built so that care not received cannot be mistaken for disease not present.

Prototype Provisional coefficients, not fitted to the cohort. Not for clinical use — do not enter patient-identifiable information.

What this prototype is for

This is the customer-discovery artefact for milestone M3. It exists so a gastroenterologist can be shown a working report and asked one question: would you act on this, and where would it sit in your clinic? Every number on it is provisional.

The one idea worth arguing about. The published Disease Severity Index assigns 26 of its 100 ulcerative-colitis points to items that record care received — advanced therapy, corticosteroids, hospitalisation — rather than disease biology. Where access to that care is unequal, those items carry the care pathway into the severity score. GERI removes them from severity and reports them separately as the Access-Contamination Index, so a reassuring score that rests on treatment never offered becomes visible rather than silent.

Try it. Load the equity-quadrant case, then change Biologic or immunomodulator from “Never used” to “Used, no improvement.” The severity barely moves; the ACI collapses. That difference is the whole argument.

Reproducibility. The scoring here mirrors reference/geri_score.py exactly; uv run reference/geri_score.py --check-parity reference/fixtures.json proves it. Coefficients live in coefficients.json with the published source of every value. The full specification is in GutEquity_Index_Specification.md.

Ancestry, not race. Genetic ancestry proportion enters the biology layers. Self-identified race enters nothing; it is collected only alongside the access variables, where it indexes exposure to unequal care rather than biology.
